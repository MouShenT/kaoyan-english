长难句分析:
   ①The American spymaster who built the Office of Strategic Services in the World War Ⅱand later laid the roots for
the CIA was fascinated with information.
  这个句子的特点是定语从句中含有两个并列的分句。该句子的主干是：The American spymaster...was fascinated
with information，主语后是who 引导的定语从句，对其修饰限制。定语从句中由and 连接的两个分句是：built the
